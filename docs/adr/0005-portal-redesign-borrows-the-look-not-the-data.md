# The portal redesign borrows the pastel reference's look, not its data

The logged-in portal (`apps/web` dashboard, calendar, insights, symptoms, settings) is being redesigned into a new visual world taken from a pastel wellness reference: soft tinted cards, large headlines, illustrated cards, a cycle-journey stepper and a progress ring. This replaces the 2026-10-17 "refine, not rebrand" choice. Every number, label and card in the new look must be filled from data Cycle Circle actually has (cycle day, phase, predictions, confidence, logged symptoms, streaks, phase-tagged articles), so blocks in the reference that need data we don't hold (an energy percentage, personal hormone levels, water intake, breathing sessions, a journal) are re-cast onto our real data or left out; they are not drawn with invented values. We chose this over copying the reference whole because a health product showing a made-up "Energy 68%" or "your hormones" chart would mislead users and make medical claims we can't back up; new features like water tracking or a journal need their own spec and data model first. Decided 2026-10-18.
